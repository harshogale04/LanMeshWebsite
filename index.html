Privacy

Stay on your network

There is no cloud in the middle. Every connection between devices is secured with TLS 1.3 and each device gets its own cryptographic identity. Your messages never leave your local network.

TLS 1.3 transportNo central serverLocal history only

How it works

Up and running in three steps

1

Join the same network

Use the same WiFi or switch on a phone hotspot. No internet needed.

2

Open LanMesh

Pick a nickname. Nearby devices appear on their own, or connect with a QR code.

3

Start talking

Chat in a group, send a voice note, or tap the camera to start a call.
